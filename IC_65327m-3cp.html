SMC65327M-3CP

Product Number: 65327M-3CP

The SMC65327-3 and SMC65327-4 are tri-state and 4-state cut-code decoder ICs with 12 address and data bits. Multiple variants are available for different applications. They are made in a high-voltage CMOS process with extremely low power consumption, and are used with the SMC65326-3 and SMC65326-4 transmitters.

The SMC65327-3 has up to 10 address input pins and 6 data output pins. Address pins can be set to 0, 1 or OPEN (tri-state). Because the default address setting is 3 (tri-state), unconnected (OPEN) pins are automatically encoded as 3.

The SMC65327-4 has up to 10 address input pins and 6 data output pins. Address pins can be set to 0, 1, 3 (tri-state) or 4TH (4-state). Because the default address setting is 3 (tri-state), unconnected pins are automatically encoded as tri-state. In the 18-pin package, pin limits mean A0 is not brought out and pin 1 is used for 4TH (4-state), so A0 is always encoded as 3 (tri-state).

Features:

  • 1. Operating voltage: 4.0–13.0 V.
  • 2. Very low quiescent power consumption (operating approx. 300 μA).
  • 3. Highly stable oscillation: with the same external resistor, frequency variation between ICs—and even between production lots—is within ±3% to ±5%.
  • 4. A full range of encoder ICs for diverse applications.
  • 5. The SMC65327L-3 and SMC65327L-4 series are fully compatible with, and drop-in replacements for, the AX5327P-3 and AX5327P-4, supporting up to 6 data bits (the AX5327P-3 and AX5327P-4 support only 4).
  • 6. The SMC65327M-3 and SMC65327M-4 series are fully compatible with, and drop-in replacements for, the AX5327P-C3 and AX5327P-C4, supporting up to 6 data bits (the AX5327-4 and AX5327P-4 support only 4).
  • 7. Security codes (2-data application): tri-state (18-pin) up to 3¹⁰ = 59,049 codes; 4-state (20-pin) up to 4¹⁰ = 1,048,576 codes.
