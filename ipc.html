IPC · Kiosk

Check-in and payment kiosks, designed and built in-house

Our self-service check-in and payment kiosk runs unattended every day at parcel pickup stores of Taiwan's largest retail system. SMC designs the enclosure, payment modules, touchscreen software and back-end integration in-house.

Check-in and payment kiosk

In service at parcel pickup stores of Taiwan's largest retail system. Designed, assembled and tested in-house; enclosure colour, branding and modules are customized for each customer.

  • Portrait touchscreen with guided UI
  • Receipt printer
  • Coin acceptor and change
  • Metal PIN pad and scanner
  • Bill acceptor and change dispenser
  • Coin and note return tray
  • Industrial PC inside, remote monitoring ready

What the kiosk does

Everything a customer needs to check in, pay and collect a receipt, in one cabinet.

Touch

Touchscreen check-in

Large portrait touchscreen with a guided flow: the customer enters a phone number, confirms the order and pays.

Cash

Bill acceptor & change dispenser

Accepts NT$100 / 500 / 1000 notes and returns change through the same note slot.

Coin

Coin acceptor & coin return

Coin input for NT$1 / 5 / 10 / 50, with a combined coin and note return tray.

Receipt

Receipt printer

Prints a receipt for every transaction from a dedicated slot.

Input

PIN pad & scanner

Metal keypad and a scanning window for barcodes or QR codes.

RFID / NFC

Card and tag readers

SMC's own RFID / NFC modules can be built in for member cards, NFC payment or staff access.

Inside the kiosk

Mechanical design is done in-house. Every module sits on its own rail or bracket and is reached from the front, so field service takes minutes.

Countertop self-service kiosk

A compact version for counters and service desks, also used as our Pandora edge AI demo kiosk.

  • 4:3 touchscreen on a compact base
  • Receipt printer behind a front service door
  • 2D barcode and QR code scanner
  • Card slot for card reading
  • Bill acceptor for cash payment
  • Fits an industrial PC or a Pandora edge AI computer

Parking payment kiosk

The same platform also runs as a parking pay station. Illustration of the configuration.

  • Search by licence plate number on the touchscreen
  • Cash payment with coin and note change
  • Contactless stored-value and NFC cards
  • Receipt and e-invoice printing
