SMC52BM50 Bluetooth module

Product Number：SMC52BM50

The REALTEK Bluetooth chip used in the SMC52BM50 Bluetooth module is an ultra-low-power system-on-a-chip for Bluetooth low-power applications, integrating an RF transceiver with excellent performance and leading technology, ARM Cortex M0, 256KB eflash, 80KB RAM, various powerful technical functions and rich peripheral interfaces. In addition, it also has built-in hardware Keyscan and Quad-Decoder functions on the module.
