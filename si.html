SI · Wireless IoT

Wireless IoT system integration, from tag to dashboard

SMC designs its own RFID, NFC and Bluetooth chips and modules, and connects them with handhelds, gateways, edge AI and cloud software into systems that run in the field. Today we serve Taiwan's largest retail system.

Wireless technologies we integrate

We pick the right radio for each job and make the devices work together in one system.

UHF RFID

Long-range bulk reading

Read dozens of tagged crates, totes or assets at once with handheld or fixed UHF readers.

HF / NFC 13.56 MHz

Cards, phones and tags

Access cards, MIFARE and ISO15693 tags, NFC on smartphones, and Apple / Google Wallet passes.

LF 125 / 134.2 kHz

Animals and harsh environments

Ear tags, glass biochips and underground markers that read reliably near metal, water and soil.

BLE

Phones and battery devices

Bluetooth modules and beacons that link readers, locks and sensors to mobile apps.

2.4 GHz active RFID

Real-time location

Battery tags read from tens of metres for personnel and asset positioning.

Wi-Fi · 4G / 5G

Backhaul to the cloud

Handhelds and gateways sync over Wi-Fi on site and 4G / 5G on the road, with offline queues.

What the crate system solves

Shipping

No wrong shipments

Crates are bulk-scanned at loading and checked against the delivery order. A mismatch needs supervisor approval before the truck leaves.

Delivery

Proof of delivery

Crates are scanned again at the customer, who signs on the handheld. Photos are saved with time and GPS.

Returns

Empty crate returns

Returned crates are scanned and signed for, and only crates registered to that customer are accepted.

Alerts

Crates at customers

A live count of crates at each customer, with overdue crates flagged after a configurable number of days.

Crate lifecycle

Each crate records its status, location, holder, last scan time and device, contents, barcode, delivery signature and return signature.

In stockIn the warehouse, on the line or in a staging area
PackedLinked to a delivery order and its contents
In transitLoading check passed; the driver becomes the holder
At customerSigned for by the customer; days outstanding start counting
ReturningSigned back by the customer and checked in at the factory
