Case: UHF RFID tracking for returnable crates

A stand-alone system that follows every crate from packing to customer delivery and back, using Android UHF RFID handhelds. It manages only the crates, so it goes live quickly without ERP integration.

Shipping

No wrong shipments

Crates are bulk-scanned at loading and checked against the delivery order. Missing or extra crates show instantly, and a mismatch needs supervisor approval before the truck leaves.

Delivery

Proof of delivery

Crates are scanned again at the customer, who signs on the handheld. Photos are saved with time and GPS.

Returns

Empty crate returns

Returned crates are scanned and signed for. Only crates registered to that customer are accepted, then checked back in at the factory.

Alerts

Crates at customers

A live count of crates at each customer, with overdue crates flagged after a configurable number of days.

Crate lifecycle

Each crate records its status, location, holder, last scan time and device, contents, barcode, delivery signature and return signature.

In stockIn the warehouse, on the line or in a staging area
PackedLinked to a delivery order and its contents
In transitLoading check passed; the driver becomes the holder
At customerSigned for by the customer; days outstanding start counting
ReturningSigned back by the customer and checked in at the factory

How the crate system is built

Handheld app

UHF RFID handhelds

Bulk crate scanning, 1D barcode backup, signature and photo capture, and an offline queue that syncs when the signal returns.

Back end

API server

Order-to-scan matching, crate status tracking, supervisor PIN or remote approval, overdue alerts and user roles.

Console

Database and web console

Crate lookup, outstanding-crate reports, exception handling and electronic delivery notes.
