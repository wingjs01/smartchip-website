iHoin Car Locator Charger

iHoin Smart Car Charger

The iHoin Car Locator Charger is a smart charger that not only charges USB devices but also saves your car's location in Google Maps. Its two USB ports deliver a combined 4.8 A, enough to charge an iPhone and an iPad at the same time. It monitors battery voltage in real time and flashes a red light when the voltage is too low, helping you keep track of your car battery's health. The companion app also records travel speed, altitude and parking time.
