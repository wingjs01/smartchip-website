Active Transceiver

Product Number: SMC-AR24

The SMC-AR24 is a receiver for active RFID tags. It accepts a wide input voltage range (DC 7–24 V), offers multiple output modes (RS232/RS485/Wiegand), and supports selectable frequency bands and data rates. Paired with SMC-AT01 active tags, it enables long-range RFID applications of about 100 m and reads up to 100 tags per second.
