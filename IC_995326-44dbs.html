SMC995326-44DBS

Product Number: 995326-44DBS

The SMC995326-34/44Dxx are 3/4-state, 4-data, no-cut-code encoder ICs available in multiple variants for different applications. Made in a CMOS process with extremely low power consumption, they are decoded on the receiver side by an MCU. Each contains 12 address and data bits, which are encoded and output serially through DOUT, with up to 4 data bits (selectable from 1 to 4) and up to 12 address bits. The address bits are programmed automatically by computer during production, so every IC ships with a fixed, unchangeable address—its security code.

Features:

  • 1. Operating voltage: 2.3–13.5 V.
  • 2. The SMC995326-34DXX is a tri-state, 4-data, no-cut-code encoder IC.
  • 3. The SMC995326-44DXX is a 4-state, 4-data, no-cut-code encoder IC.
  • 4. Very low quiescent power consumption (standby approx. 1–3 μA, operating approx. 600 μA).
  • 5. Highly stable oscillation: with the same external resistor, frequency variation between ICs—and even between production lots—is within ±3% to ±5%.
  • 6. Simple PCB layout on a single-layer board, reducing overall product cost.
  • 7. Each IC's security code is programmed before shipment, so no code cutting is needed on the production line.
  • 8. A full range of encoder ICs for diverse applications.
  • 9. Special-function no-cut-code encoder ICs reduce external components.
  • 10. The SMC995326-34Dxx series is fully compatible with, and a drop-in replacement for, the AX5326-3.
  • 11. The SMC995326-44Dxx series is fully compatible with, and a drop-in replacement for, the AX5326-4.
  • 12. A special design gives the 16-pin SMC995326-34Dxx four data ports.
  • 13. A special design gives the 16-pin SMC995326-44Dxx four data ports.
  • 14. If the SMC995326-3 (tri-state) does not provide enough codes, simply swap in the SMC995326-4 (4-state) without changing the PCB—reducing inventory and material planning.
