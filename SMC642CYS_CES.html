SMC642CYS / 642CZS Note: Customization is available. Operating frequency, interface, communication protocol and operating mode can be tailored to customer requirements.

Product Number: 642CYS / 642CZS

The SMC642CYS/CZS series is a 13.56 MHz dual-antenna contactless reader module for ISO14443A and ISO15693. It is built on the SMC642, a 2-in-1 decoding control chip that combines an RF front end and a control interface, and is designed to read SMC4111 UIDs and read/write ISO14443A and ISO15693 cards.

You do not need to learn the complex control commands of RF chips such as the RC522/RC500/RC400/RC632, or deal with MIFARE key management and access control. Just three simple commands (Read Data, Write Data, Change Key) let you master MIFARE read/write operations in the shortest time. Anti-collision is handled automatically by the module, so even beginners can finish development quickly, shortening development time and reducing development and overall product costs. The series provides UART/I2C and UART/SPI interfaces for direct communication with an MCU board.
