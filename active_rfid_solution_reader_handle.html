Product Overview

The SMC-HR24 is a portable 2.4 GHz Active RFID Reader designed for mobile inspections, rapid inventory checks, and on-site verification. Lightweight and ergonomic, it works seamlessly with active RFID tags for applications such as warehouse management, equipment tracking, personnel identification, and field inspection. Equipped with a -90 dBm high-sensitivity receiver and 4 dBi antenna, the SMC-HR24 provides stable mid-to-long range tag detection. It supports USB, Bluetooth, and optional RS232 interfaces for easy integration with various backend systems. Powered by a rechargeable battery, the device enables efficient mobile operations in both indoor and outdoor environments. Compact, durable, and easy to operate, the SMC-HR24 is an ideal solution for mobile RFID identification and field applications.
