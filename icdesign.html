Rooted in Silicon

Since 1995, SMC RFID chips have held a high market share worldwide.

SMC started in 1995 as an ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit) design house. Serving a wide range of customer requirements, it built deep expertise and developed its own RFID chips, which have held a high global market share ever since.

R&D Since 1995

Drawing on decades of chip design and module development, SMC developed the SMC522 chip, the core of all recent SMC products.
