Product Overview

SMC’s self-developed 2.4GHz active tag adopts a proprietary 2.4GHz wireless protocol and supports bidirectional communication, enabling functions such as data transmission, LED indication, and audible alerts. With an ultra-low-power design and highly optimized RF control mechanism, the tag delivers stable reading performance with a battery life of up to three years. With up to 3 years of battery life, S-TAG is ideal for asset management, warehouse logistics, personnel tracking, equipment monitoring, and any application requiring long-range detection.

Key Features

  • • 2.4GHz active wireless communication for extended read range (up to 0–30m in open space).
  • • Ultra-low power consumption, with an average standby current of only 15uA (CR2450 battery lasts up to 3 years).
  • • Bidirectional communication enabling remote control of LED and buzzer for search and alert functions.
  • • High-capacity multi-tag identification, suitable for high-density logistics environments.
  • • Battery status reporting for easier system maintenance.
  • • Frequency hopping (2450/2455/2460MHz) to ensure stable performance in noisy RF environments.

Applications

  • 1. Logistics and warehouse management

    • Provides automated inventory, location, and reporting for inbound, outbound, and inventory management:

    • • Automated inbound/outbound processing
    • • Real-time location tracking
    • • Inventory automation with discrepancy reporting
    • • High-speed mass tag identification
  • 2. Asset Management (Check-out / Return / Tracking)
    • • One-to-one tag binding
    • • Automatic loan/return records
    • • Abnormal movement detection
    • • Multi-point transceiver positioning
  • 3. Personnel Tracking & Safety

    • Through the positioning technology of receivers and signal strength calculation, it can be used for:

    • • Indoor real-time personnel positioning
    • • Restricted-area monitoring
    • • Two-way search/alert with LED & buzzer
