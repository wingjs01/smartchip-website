Bluetooth + Dual band dual antenna NFC/RFID

This product is a perfect combination of the high frequency & low frequency & Bluetooth 4.0, and it can be connected or operated offline, and this is the best device for IOT to make connection more convenient.

Automatically store 1,000 groups of IDs in connection and offline status and automatically mark them according to the attributes of the tags. The card reading mode includes a command mode (read and write mode)

a single card mode and a continuous card reading mode, and the anti-duplication reading function automatically starts when a card is read.

This product’s read/write function conforms to most NFC tags and devices, and has a wide range of applications, such as access control, inventory management, and automatic verification of logical access control.

Basic Functions

  • 1. Protocol：Communication protocol of ISO14443A + ISO15693, 125KHz RFID, ISO11784/5, or combination of HF and LF
  • 2. Applicable System：PC & iOS & Android systems are available
  • 3. Single card reading mode will not be stored and will only be used for browsing card numbers.
  • 4. 1,000 sets of IDs can be stored. The continuous reading or single card reading mode is automatically memorized when connected/offline.
  • 5. USB, APP, and Mode Key operations can coexist
  • 6. Only PC operation software has a command mode and can read/write tags when connecting.
  • 7. There are 4 LED lights and Beep sound as an operating aid.
  • 8. There is a built-in rechargeable 500mA battery in this reader.
  • 9. Provide PC/APP operating software
  • 10. Provide API protocol. Support secondary development.
  • 11. APP functions:
    • a. The following settings must be made in the Bluetooth connection state
    • b. Protocol: can choose 14443A or 15693 or (125KHz RFID or ISO11784/5) or multiple combinations of communication protocols.
    • c. Reading mode:
      • Continuous card reading mode: Click the continuous card reading button to read the card continuously.
      • Single card reader mode: Click the single card reading button once to read the card one time.
    • d. Anti-repeat reading function: The initial setting is on, and can be changed to off. When the function is turned on, the 6 ID codes forward are automatically compared.
