High frequency Bluetooth Interface

Product Number：SMC-P135S

The SMC-P135S is a NFC smart R/W device with Bluetooth that can read tags conforming to the ISO14443A & ISO15693 communication protocol. This product combines 13.56MHz & Bluetooth 4.0 perfectly and can be On-line & Off- Line operation, this is the best device for implementing IOT to make networking easier.

BLE4.0 communication of interface SMC-P135S can be used under iOS & Android or offline.，Automatically store 1,000 groups of IDs in connection and offline status and automatically mark ISO14443A or ISO15693 according to the attributes of the tags. The card reading mode includes a command mode (read and write mode), a single card mode and a continuous card reading mode, and the anti-duplication reading function automatically starts when a card is read.

This product’s read/write function conforms to most NFC tags and devices, and has a wide range of applications, such as access control, inventory management, and automatic verification of logical access control.

Basic Functions：

  • 1. Protocol：ISO14443A + ISO15693。
  • 2. Applicable System：PC & iOS & Android systems are available
  • 3. Single card reading mode will not be saved and is only used for browsing card number.
  • 4. 1,000 sets of IDs can be stored. The continuous reading or single card reading mode is automatically memorized when connected/offline.
  • 5. Operation authority USB > APP = Mode Key。
  • 6. Only the PC operating software has the command mode function, and the tag can be read and written when the USB is connected.
  • 7. There are 4 LED lights and Beep sound as an operating aid.
  • 8. There is a built-in rechargeable 500mA battery in this reader.
  • 9. After charging, the standby time is about 10 months or longer, and continuous using time is about 1 month.
  • 10. it can read more than 500,000 times a day
  • 11. Provide PC/APP operating software
  • 12. Provide API protocol. Support secondary development
  • 13. APP functions：
    • a. The following settings must be made in the Bluetooth connection state.
    • b. Protocol: can choose 14443A or 15693 or multiple combinations of communication protocols.
    • c. Reading mode:
      • Continuous card reading mode: Click the continuous card reading button to read the card continuously.
      • Single card reader mode: Click the single card reading button once to read the card one time.
    • d. Anti-repeat reading function: Automatically compares 6 sets of IDs forward, if there are duplicates, it will not be stored, and the duplicated card numbers will be distinguished by color.
