Pets Management Solution

Pet has become an indispensable part of modern people's life. With the rapid increase in the number of pets, pet supervision and safety issues have become increasingly prominent, to prevent the loss of pets, abandonment and disease transmission and other issues, SMC pet management solution (pet chip, syringe , hand-code reader) is the best combination.

Handheld Reader：

Used to read ISO11784 / 5, FDX-B products, the reader has a large display screen to read the information clear, easy to read, a single button, with automatic energy saving, easy to operate, provide USB signal transmission, 9V battery- Can be used for three months. (Optional with external power supply)

Read Only Type Biochip Specifications：

Standard operating frequency: 134.2KHz.

The animal identification code format conforms to ISO11784 / 5, FDX-B standard.

Global unique serial number to ensure the uniqueness of animal wafer serial numbers.

Size: diameter <=2 .1mm, length <=1 2mm

Material: all from lead-free non-toxic or medical equipment grade material production.

Effectively prevent static breakdown, pressure more than 5000v.

Working temperature: 0 ℃ ~ 50 ℃.

Storage temperature: -40 ℃ ~ 80 ℃.

Duration: Data saved for more than 15 years.
