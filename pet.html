Pet Management Solution

Pets have become an indispensable part of modern life. As pet numbers grow rapidly, pet supervision and safety are becoming increasingly important. To prevent lost and abandoned pets and the spread of disease, SMC's pet management solution—pet microchips, syringes and handheld readers—is the ideal combination.

Handheld Reader:

Reads ISO11784/5 FDX-B tags. The large display shows information clearly, and single-button operation with automatic power saving makes it easy to use. It supports USB data transfer and runs for about three months on a 9 V battery (external power optional).

Read-Only Biochip Specifications:

Standard operating frequency: 134.2 kHz.

The animal ID code format complies with ISO11784/5 FDX-B.

Globally unique serial number for every animal chip.

Size: diameter ≤ 2.1 mm, length ≤ 12 mm

Material: lead-free, non-toxic or medical-grade materials.

ESD protection: over 5,000 V.

Working temperature: 0 ℃ ~ 50 ℃.

Storage temperature: -40 ℃ ~ 80 ℃.

Data retention: over 15 years.

Read/Write Biochip Specifications:

The animal ID code format complies with ISO11784/5 FDX-B.

Globally unique serial number for every animal chip.

Memory of over 128 bits for storing official registration data.

Data can be read and written more than 100,000 times.

Size: diameter ≤ 2.1 mm, length ≤ 12 mm

Material: lead-free, non-toxic or medical-grade materials.

ESD protection: over 5,000 V.

Working temperature: 0 ℃ ~ 50 ℃.

Storage temperature: -40 ℃ ~ 80 ℃.

Data retention: over 15 years.

Supplied in a disposable syringe meeting national standards, sealed in transparent paper packaging.
