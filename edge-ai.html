Edge AI · Pandora

Generative AI at the edge with Pandora

Pandora is a compact AI computer built on NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX Super. SMC supplies it and builds it into complete edge AI systems together with our RFID, NFC and kiosk products.

Pandora at a glance

Key facts. Contact us for the full datasheet and the configuration that fits your project.

PlatformNVIDIA Jetson Orin NX Super
AI performanceUp to 157 TOPS (16 GB model)
SoftwareUbuntu with NVIDIA JetPack SDK, ready for mainstream AI frameworks and models
Form factorCompact desktop unit, about 470 g
Front I/OUSB 3.2, USB 3.2 / OTG, power button

What you can run on it

Run models locally, with no cloud round-trip, low latency and data that stays on site.

Pandora in action

At the Project Pandora exhibition, one Pandora ran three live demos inside an SMC countertop kiosk.

One kiosk, three AI applications at once

The Edge AI self-service kiosk has a single Pandora inside, yet smart logistics, smart retail and face detection with people-flow analytics all stay running on it.
