ISO14443A Reader module RFID + BLE 4.0

Product Number：SMC5220S-R

SMC5220S is a low-frequency RFID module integrated with BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) transmission. It uses standard Bluetooth protocols as a bridge to transmit RFID data and commands to various smart devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops. The module incorporates the original SMC522 series chip , featuring a streamlined command set, simplified development process, and cost-oriented design. It is ideal for advanced applications such as device development and reader integration, as well as for users involved in the design and production of RFID card terminals.
