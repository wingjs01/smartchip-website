Solution

Electronic Anti-Theft System for Motorcycles

SMC's RFID electronic key applies RFID technology to vehicle security. When the key is turned ON, the lock reads the key's signal and starts the motorcycle only if the signal matches. A thief using a key without the matching chip cannot start the engine, providing effective theft protection.

Beacon Control

Use Bluetooth beacons to control devices and receive commands. The beacon broadcasts data packets at regular intervals, and an independent Bluetooth host (such as a mobile phone) receives them.

Bluetooth + Dual-Band, Dual-Antenna NFC/RFID

This product combines HF, LF and Bluetooth 4.0 and works both connected and offline, making IoT connectivity easy. It stores up to 1,000 IDs automatically whether connected or offline and labels each ID by tag type. Reading modes include command mode (read/write)

2.4 GHz Real-Time Positioning Solution

Active RFID transmitter modules and 2.4 GHz readers work together to locate targets within a defined range. The system can be used for personnel positioning, livestock tracking, tool tracking and more, and works with SMC's database platform to improve operational and management efficiency.

Livestock Management Solution

RFID livestock management lets farmers monitor the growth and health of cattle, sheep and pigs. Early analysis and treatment help detect health problems in advance, prevent outbreaks and support food-safety management.

Pet Management Solution

Pets have become an indispensable part of modern life. As pet numbers grow rapidly, pet supervision and safety are becoming increasingly important. To prevent lost and abandoned pets and the spread of disease, SMC's pet management solution—pet microchips, syringes and handheld readers—is the ideal combination.
