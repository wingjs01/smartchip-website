Livestock Management Solutions

RFID livestock management lets farmers monitor the growth and health of cattle, sheep and pigs. Early analysis and treatment help detect health problems in advance, prevent outbreaks and support food-safety management.

Epidemics can break out anywhere at any time, and consumers care more and more about food safety. With SMC's RFID livestock management suite, farms can track information across breeding, slaughtering, processing, packaging, transport and retail, making the production process transparent. This protects consumers by ensuring meat quality and safety, and makes livestock products more competitive.

Advantages of RFID ear tags:

Patented compact design that resists biting, for long-term use.

Safe, non-toxic and inspection-compliant for effective management.

Long read range; waterproof; resistant to high/low temperatures and acids.

Unaffected by feces, urine or temperature changes.

RFID ear tag specifications:

Standard: complies with ISO11784/5 FDX-B.

Materials: all lead-free, non-toxic, non-polluting, environmentally friendly materials.

Storage temperature: -40 to 80 ℃.

Housing: withstands high and low temperatures (100 ℃ for 10 minutes), sun and rain.

Ammonia test: withstands immersion in 20% ammonia solution for 12 hours or more.
