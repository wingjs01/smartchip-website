2.4 GHz Real-Time Positioning Solution

Active RFID tags and transceivers work together to locate targets within a defined range. The system can be used for personnel positioning, livestock tracking, tool tracking and more, and works with SMC's database platform to improve operational and management efficiency.

The real-time positioning system runs on SMC's own platform to monitor a variety of applications. Managers can view tag distribution, movement trends and timing on the platform and manage multiple readers effectively. Different reader placements and coverage settings support a variety of positioning functions and modes, and everything is shown clearly on a floor plan, helping managers collect and manage data in one place.

Usable indoors and outdoors, compact, and suitable for a wide range of applications, including:

  • ・Big data collection: positioning data can be analyzed to inform marketing strategy.
  • ・Personnel monitoring: know where staff are in a factory—or whether they are absent—to improve efficiency and safety.
  • ・Livestock management: saves labor and tracks livestock locations to prevent loss or theft.
  • ・Finding pets and children: real-time positioning greatly shortens the search when a pet or child goes missing.
  • ・Hot-spot analytics: find popular areas from foot-traffic data and study consumer behavior.
  • ・Hazard detection: for hospitals, schools, nursing homes, prisons and more.
  • ・Adjustable detection range: set the detection radius from 1 m to 20 m to suit the space or application.

Product specifications

  • Tag: two CR2032 batteries; transmits twice per second; about 4 months of use
  • Reader: 9 V power
