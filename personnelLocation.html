2.4Ghz Real-time Positioning Solution

By active RFID tags and transceiver collocated with each other to make a specific distance positioning function, this device can be used for personnel positioning, livestock tracking, static tool control and other applications, and enhance the efficiency of work and management purposes with SMC development of the database platform.

Real-time positioning system using SMC self-architecture platform to monitor a variety of applications, and managers can use the location of the platform distribution, tag trends and time to identify the data and effectively control multi-point reader. This system can develop a variety of different positioning functions and modes with different set-up points and coverage settings, and can be displayed on the platform clearly in a plan view, which helps managers to receive data and manage them uniformly.

Can be used indoor and outdoor, small size and wide range of applications, can be used for:

  • ・Big Data Collection: The use of information collected from the positioning analysis of big data collection can effectively help the formulation of marketing strategies.
  • ・Personal Control: Can really know the movement of staff in factories or the absent of staff, helping to improve work efficiency and ensure the safety of personnel.
  • ・Livestock Management: Save manpower cost and grasp the location of livestock to prevent livestock from getting loss or theft.
  • ・Searching Pets and Children: When pets or children lose, the instant positioning system can help significantly reduce the search time.
  • ・Top locations statistics: Find hot spots by data of flow of people and then study consumer behavior.
  • ・Danger Detection: The scope of application in hospitals, schools, nursing homes, prisons, etc.
  • ・Adjustable Distance Detection: Can adjust detection distance according to different space size or needs, and the radius of the distance can be from 1 meter to 20 meters.

Product specifications

  • Reader：Power: 9V
